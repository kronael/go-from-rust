## 20:20 2026-08-06

- Shipped v0.3.0: lesson 45, sum types as variant structs behind optional
  pointers. Landed after the owner rejected a flat `Kind`-discriminant design —
  a `Kind` field is a second source of truth that can disagree with the
  payload, and needs constructors purely to police itself. Pointer presence as
  the tag cannot disagree, structurally.
- Handling is independent nil checks, not a type switch: none-set and both-set
  are ordinary states, not bugs, whenever nothing requires exactly one variant.
- Companion infographic published at
  krons.fiu.wtf/pub/krons/go-sum-types/ — spine is "who keeps the tag honest",
  with `(T, error)` as the proof that Go's most-used sum type is enforced by
  nothing but convention.
